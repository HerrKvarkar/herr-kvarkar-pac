# Asset provenance

This is a personal PAC assembly and adaptation. It does not claim ownership of upstream meshes or textures, nor grant rights beyond those of the original authors.

- Base outfit supplied by the user: Alex Honda PAC save. Retained sprint and laser behavior; rebuilt the character appearance.
- DEA clothing, gloves, tie and aviators: mounted `models/player/cheddar/agent/agent_mike.mdl` and related Cheddar/Civil Networks content. Animated male07 head and facial controls were recovered from this model.
- Earpiece source: mounted `models/player/cheddar/beta1_agent/beta_1_agent.mdl`; extracted, retargeted and fitted as a separate accessory.
- Overcoat: [CalvinLucien-doid/PAC3, overcoat2.zip](https://github.com/CalvinLucien-doid/PAC3/blob/main/overcoat2.zip), long coat body variant; original contributors retain their rights.
- Hair style 1: [maliayoruko/PAC3hosting, MaleHairstyles.zip](https://github.com/maliayoruko/PAC3hosting/blob/main/MaleHairstyles.zip), male07 hair pack; original contributors retain their rights.
- Blue iris/cornea/ambient textures: mounted `models/icpd/engine` and `models/icpd/pig_shared` content.
- Laser model: mounted `models/weapons/tfa_ins2/upgrades/a_laser_mak.mdl` and its material dependencies.
- Source/Valve human skeleton, eye shaders and player animation framework remain game dependencies.
- Facial-control recovery used [Crowbar Command Line](https://github.com/UltraTechX/Crowbar-Command-Line), based on ZeqMacaw's Crowbar. The executable is not bundled here.
- Custom face and red shirt diffuse masters were edited with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool; VTF encoding and model assembly used local tooling.
- V2 field-trouser and boot source: mounted `models/player/cheddar/omega1/omega1_officer.mdl`, retargeted together to preserve their original ankle joins. Black trouser and leather glove/coat diffuse variants use the built-in image tool; source authors retain their rights.
- V2 embossed External Affairs emblem: [user-provided DEA reference](https://postimg.cc/vcVFVVKV), incorporated as shallow geometry relief with the original leather UVs and skin weights.
- Coat-tail procedural-bone setup was checked against [Valve's Source SDK jiggle-bone implementation](https://github.com/ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013/blob/master/src/public/jigglebones.cpp).

ISD scales-of-justice emblem: original image supplied through the user-linked [ISD roster](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uLWo99ymBUu331g9Cp41uwtpEi11X3tVsuxYWMBZ3gE/edit?gid=704543577), used unchanged as the alpha silhouette of the conforming back relief. No roster data is included.
